Within the family Lamiaceae, two distinct perennial taxa exhibit documented in-vitro bactericidal and mucolytic efficacy across respiratory pathways: Holy Basil (Ocimum tenuiflorum) and Garden Sage (Salvia officinalis).

Their therapeutic bioactivity is governed by concentrated volatile monoterpenes and lipophilic phenolic diterpenes. In both matrices, the steam-distilled volatile fraction acts directly on bacterial wall integrity while stimulating localized ciliary clearance in respiratory epithelia.
