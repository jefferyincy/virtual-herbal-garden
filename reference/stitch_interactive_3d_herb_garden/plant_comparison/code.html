CRITICAL MORPHOLOGICAL DISTINCTION

These plants are sometimes confused in the wild — see look-alike notes

Ocimum tenuiflorum features purple-tinted pubescent (hairy) stems, sharply toothed leaf margins, and highly pungent clove-musk aroma. Common sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum) possesses entirely glabrous (smooth) grooved green stems with cupped oval leaves and sweet anethole/clove fragrance. Never substitute non-medicinal culinary cultivars for clinical adaptogenic regimens.
